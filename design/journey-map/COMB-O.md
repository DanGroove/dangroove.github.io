## What was measured (lag-8, on the PC, Chrome 157 headless, 1920x950 DPR 2, 66 runs)

A craft press, the 2.5 s after the click, longest frame: explore 32 ms, steps (the compass focused from the map beat) 30 to 40 ms with three or four frames over 25 ms. With `applyMask`'s dim switched off (`findhoards.mjs`) the press's longest frame falls to 10 to 12 ms in both. The compass lines and the find bits and flight barely matter. The steps pull-back (`stepsFocus` with `wayResultView`) adds the later long frames in steps mode: svg#map rebases when the zoom leaves the hold's `[scaleMin, scaleMax]` band.

Why the dim costs that much: `applyMask` changes the opacity of `#b-all` (inside svg#map-base) and `#b-rest` (inside svg#map), every find's `--jm-find` (a 0.3 s transition, inside svg#map), `#bc-notes` and the unlit hoards. Opacity on an SVG child is never composited, so the press invalidates almost every tile of both map layers in one frame (on Dan's 4K screen one full re-raster of svg#map costs about 30 ms), and the finds' transition keeps re-rastering their regions for 0.3 s.

**E1. Sköll and Hati: the eclipse (`depth.mjs` the sky only, `data.mjs` `DEPTH.wolves` and `DEPTH.drawings` sky entries, `map.css` `#sky` rules).** In Norse myth, when Sköll or Hati catches the sun or moon, that is an eclipse. Each run now builds to one catch. Keep the approved wolf drawings, colours and gallop; keep leg 2 Sköll and sun, leg 4 Hati and moon, forward moves only, never in explore or reduced motion, the Back fade as now.
- Where: in the open sky right of the map, not over its labels. Each frame, `edge` = the screen x of the map frame's right edge (`DEPTH.frame.outer[2]` through the camera) plus `gap` (24 px). The run goes right to left from `W + m` (as now) to `L = max(edge, W - maxBand x W)` (`maxBand` 0.55). The arc formula stays (`span` from the band's two ends, `arcH = min(arcH[0] x H, arcH[1] x span)`, `yTop`). When `W - L < minBand` (360 px) the band is too narrow (phones; a squat window): use today's full-width arc instead, blending the two over 120 px so nothing jumps.
- When: per leg, in glide place as now: Sköll `window: [0.36, 0.98]`, Hati `window: [0.02, 0.66]` at PC sizes (measured 2026-11-26: the open sky beside the map is at least 300 px wide over those parts of each glide at 1440x900 and 1920x950); phones keep `[0.06, 0.94]`. `u` (0 to 1) is the run's own progress inside the window; fade in over the first 0.1 of `u`, out over the last 0.1, and also fade out as the lead reaches `L`.
- Bigger sun and moon with light: disc radius `R = sunR x s` (`sunR` 18, so about 30 px at 1440 wide; the moon the same), rays and crescent scaled to match. Behind each, a soft glow (its own small element, a radial gradient: the sun `C.warm`, the moon `C.paper`; radius `7R`, alpha 0.3 for the sun, 0.16 for the moon) that moves with it.
- The chase: from `u` 0.08 to 0.75 the wolf throws off motes from its back: Sköll warm sparks (`C.warm` and a lighter warm), Hati pale frost motes (`C.paper` at 0.7), 8 to 12 a second, each living 0.5 to 0.8 s, drifting back along the path and a little up, shrinking and fading. They live in one small svg that travels with the wolf (never a full-screen canvas).
- The catch, `k = smoothstep(0.28, 0.46, u) x (1 - smoothstep(0.6, 0.76, u))`:
  - the gap closes: the wolf trails the lead by `lag x ws x (1 - 0.85 k)`, so at the peak its head crosses the disc;
  - the disc swells to `R x (1 + 0.5 k)`;
  - the wolf turns to a silhouette: fill `C.shade`, outline brighter (Sköll `C.warm`, Hati `C.paper`), blended by `k`;
  - the bite: a `C.shade` disc of radius `0.96 R`, clipped to the sun or moon, offset from its centre by `(1 - 0.55 k) x 2R` toward the side the wolf comes from (fully off the disc at `k` 0);
  - a ring of light: a crisp circle at `R + 1.5` px (1.4 px) and a blurred one at `R + 7` px, opacity `k` (the sun's warm white, the moon's `C.paper`);
  - the sky dims a little: one oversized element (at least 3W x 3H) with a radial gradient from clear inside `2R` to `C.shade` at 0.5 alpha at its edge, centred on the disc by a CSS transform each frame, opacity `0.5 x k`. Never a flash: it eases in and out with `k` only;
  - Hati's moon only: the crescent fades out and the moon's ghost disc fills dark grey (`#5A5652`) by `k`, and about 18 small star sparkles (`C.paper`) in its glow fade in with `k` between 2.5R and 6R from it.
- After the peak the lead slips free (the gap reopens to `1.15 x lag x ws` by `u` 0.85) and both run on to the band's end.
- Cost: every new piece is its own small element moved by CSS transform and faded by opacity, `will-change: transform` only while visible, `visibility: hidden` at alpha 0. Only the wolf (legs, bob, fill blend), the lead (during the catch) and the motes re-raster per frame, each small. The glow and the dim change only transform and opacity. Nothing full-screen re-rasters per frame.
