# Journey map: Steps build

22 November 2026. Implemented the Steps default, the Scroll comparison mode and the Lennard band card. Browser review is still required.

## Files

- `data.mjs`: the prescribed pace, input thresholds, navigation sizes and draft copy; Lennard's exact section 12 palette. The frame cap and first-show fade duration also live in `STEPS`.
- `pace.mjs` (new): ten beats derived from `SEQ`, linear pace parts, progress/time conversion, move duration and ramp easing. It imports in Node without a DOM.
- `steps.mjs` (new): owns progress in both modes; advances moves and jump fades from the existing frame loop; handles hurry, reversal, direct scrubbing, find returns, inputs, the stepper, announcements, review switch, saved mode and `window.__jm`.
- `main.mjs`: routes stops and timeline hooks through the controller, reads its progress once per frame, reserves navigation space on resize and resets the clock after visibility changes. `JOIN.heroStart` lets the join hide the stepper at the top.
- `trip.mjs`: the rush camera starts at the last leg's smoothed centre and eases to the X. The act table and other trip calculations retain their meaning and values.
- `cards.mjs`: passes the three optional palette values, shares the existing arrow markup and positions/sizes cards around the stepper.
- `finds.mjs`: captures inaccessible finds, samples legs every 2 vh, jumps to the best view and replays the original kind of press once. A return position belongs to the controller. Pointer aiming is held until that visit; the beacon also accepts Enter/Space in Steps.
- `footer.mjs`: adds the X's accessible round hit area, enables it only in the specified interval and routes its action through the controller. Footer label copy now comes from data.
- `map.css`: Steps page locking, touch behavior, navigation, jump cover, review switch, phone timeline offset and Lennard's weight/chip styling.
- `join.mjs`: uses controller progress, signals the joined top, swaps the hero cue at runtime and dispatches plain scroll events after lift changes in Steps.
- `join.css`: adds cover/stepper layers and the inherited hero start-button style.
- `tools/check.mjs`: validates all Steps numbers, ten rising beats, the exact Lennard palette, optional keys and import style. It also checks camera continuity numerically at the X.
- `tools/steps-test.mjs` (new): the required Node-only pace, inverse and neighboring-move tests.
- `joined.html`, `dist/journey-map.html`, `dist/journey-joined.html`: regenerated by the unchanged pack tools.
- `STEPS-BUILD.md` (new): this implementation and review record.

`timeline.mjs` needed no edit: its two existing hooks now reach the controller through `main.mjs`. No copied hero, reference, asset or protected source file was changed.

## Reference constraints

Read STEPS, the named runtime files, BRIEF, JOIN and both previous build records; studied both approved reference sheets. Acceptance criteria were to preserve the detailed map, standing-stone/card relationship, branded card skeleton, footer hierarchy and existing trip state, while replacing only the progress source and reserving space for navigation. Lennard returns to its white logo band over orange, with Warm Gray 11 as its only dark.

## Departure and limits

One extension to section 8: when a desktop view is too short for a 420 px card plus both required clearances, its height is capped and its face can scroll internally. Moving its centre alone cannot preserve both the 12 px top clearance and the 12 px stepper clearance in that case. Other implementation requirements were followed without deliberate departures.

No browser was opened. Rendered comparisons against the references, interaction behavior, focus, announcements, real touch, offline runtime errors and motion quality have not been verified. The Node results establish numerical/source checks, not a visual or accessibility pass. No independent design critique was run, as requested.
